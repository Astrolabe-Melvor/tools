Making money

Every way your account can make gold right now, ranked by what you would earn if you left it running. Click a method for what it produces, what it uses and the gear it assumes.

ActionGold in 8 hrGold/hr XP/hrSuccessSec/actionNotes
How this is calculated

Every active money method in one list: gathering, hunting, production (including herblore), thieving and heists, agility and combat. Gold = everything produced valued at its shop sell price, plus coins picked up. With ingredients counted, the cost of what you use (at sell price) is subtracted: ingredients, traps, course entry fees, and for combat your food, ammo and runes. "Gold in N hr" stops when your bank runs out of what the method uses. Applied: equipped toolbelt gear, shop upgrades, pets, mastery guild ranks and astrology nodes. Combat uses your best saved loadout per enemy (see the Combat tab for detail). Hunting uses the best trap you own and your journal level per animal. Agility uses your technique levels and assumes no shortcuts built. Farming and ranching run alongside everything else, so they have their own button and a running total above the list: each plot uses the crop you have planted (and suggests a better one), assuming you replant on time; each pen uses your animals' saved happiness, and counts feed, treats and the cost of replacing animals when they die of old age. Not included yet: grain mill and distillery, heist bonuses, potions you could drink. Click a row for the breakdown.

Combat

Every fight simulated with your saved loadouts: how fast you kill, what you earn and how much food it costs. Click a monster for the full breakdown.

EnemyGold/hrCombat XP/hr Slayer coins/hrSec/killHit Food/hrWorst turnNotes
How this is calculated

Simulated fights using your loadout from the save: equipment, set bonuses, stance, prayer, spell, food light, combat toolbelt items, pets and combat mastery ranks. Includes enemy special attacks and the combat triangle, elemental bonuses, area effects and darkness. Not modelled: damage over time, stuns and other status effects, conditional bonuses, potions you might drink. Gold = coins plus drops at sell price (chests valued by their contents). Slayer coins and Slayer XP only count while the enemy is your task. Food/hr assumes you auto-eat and never die. Worst turn = every hit of the enemy's strongest attack landing at max. 3 s respawn between kills. Click a row for the breakdown.

Upgrades

Shop purchases and mastery ranks, ranked by how much faster they would make you money, with how long each takes to pay for itself.

How this is calculated

Each upgrade is applied to your account and the money model is re-run for the skills it affects. Gold/hr gain = how much the best method in that skill improves, using 8-hour totals so methods your bank can't keep fed don't count. XP gain = the change in that skill's best XP/hr. Payback = cost (gold plus items at sell price) / gold/hr gain. Combat, bank space and other upgrades the money model doesn't cover show "not estimated". Only shop upgrades whose requirements you meet are listed.

Gear

How this is calculated

Every skill has its own toolbelt. For each skill this takes the method that makes the most gold over 8 hours with the best gear you own, and shows three setups for it: what you have equipped, the best gear you own (bank or any toolbelt), and the best gear in the game. Wishlist = items you don't own, each added on top of your best owned setup, ranked by gold/hr gained on that method (or XP/hr where a skill's gear only adds XP). Potions are charged at sell price per action. Slots locked behind shop upgrades or mastery ranks are skipped. Bonuses that depend on conditions or scale with something aren't counted yet. Item names link to the wiki for where to get them.

Astrology

Your constellations, when the next node slot opens, and which node to pick. Nodes are ranked by what they add to your best gold/hr and XP/hr (re-running the money model with the node added); nodes that unlock content are listed first.

How this is calculated

Knowledge only goes up. Minor node slots open at fixed knowledge totals (12, 66, 178, 290, 582, ...) and you choose which minor node fills each one; every 4 minor nodes opens a major slot, and majors can be taken in any order. Time to the next slot uses 1,200 studies of knowledge per hour plus +2% per minor node you have in that constellation (other knowledge bonuses not counted yet). Gold/hr gain is the change in the best 8-hour method of each skill the node affects; XP gain is the change in that skill's best XP/hr. Combat bonuses aren't estimated. See the wiki's constellation pages for every node and the observation odds.
